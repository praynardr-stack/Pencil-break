HOW TO BREAK
  1. Your opponent holds their pencil out with both fists. You hold yours by the point.
  2. The aim slides along their pencil. Tap to lock your spot.
  3. Swipe back and forth as fast as you can to bend your pencil back like a sling. Keep going till it lets go.
  4. The eraser end slams down. That metal band hits hard.
  5. The middle cracks easiest. Hit the same crack again to stack damage.
  6. Hit too close to their fists and the recoil cracks YOUR pencil by the eraser.
  7. When their pencil is about to go, you get ULTRA READY. Finish it on that swing and your pencil's own ULTRA finisher goes off.
  8. First pencil to snap loses the round. First to 2 rounds wins.
  9. Wins earn rank points. Climb all the way to PENCIL BREAK KING.
