Pencil Break — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Pencil Break is a game that runs on your device. This policy explains what the game does with information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information. The game has no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and no online accounts.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, unlocks and scores are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Fonts

The game may download its display fonts from Google Fonts the first time it runs. Google may receive your device's IP address as part of that standard web request, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
